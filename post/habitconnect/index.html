Habit Connect

2025-10-17 — Written by Yehsun

HabitConnect is a Habit Builder and Networking app designed specifically for job seekers to help them maintain their routines by sharing their habit lists, statistics, and networking through LinkedIn, and collaborating on Notion workspaces.

Tech stack #

TypeScript, React, Supabase, Figma, Tailwind CSS

Key Features #

  • Create habits to build (8 maximum)
  • Statistics to track completed tasks
  • Break feature allows users to set a break time
  • Social page lists users who share their LinkedIn statistics data
  • Users can optionally share and unshare their profiles on social page
  • Only profile-shared users can access social page (reasonable authorization for shared users)
  • Authentication through OAuth2 for security and convenience

Habit Tracking #

The app provides a clean and intuitive interface for tracking daily habits. Users can create up to 8 habits and monitor their progress with visual statistics.

Statistics & Analytics #

Comprehensive analytics help users understand their habit patterns and maintain consistency in their routines.

Social Networking #

The social page enables users to connect with other job seekers, share progress, and build accountability partnerships through LinkedIn integration.

Break Management #

Users can set break times to maintain a healthy work-life balance while pursuing their job search and habit-building goals.

Profile Customization #

Flexible profile sharing options allow users to control their privacy while still benefiting from the community aspects of the app.

Authentication & Security #

Secure OAuth2 authentication ensures user data is protected while providing a seamless login experience through existing social accounts.
